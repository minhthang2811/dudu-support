# Privacy Policy for DuDu Badminton

**Last updated: 13 November 2026**

DuDu Badminton is a warm-up and stretching coach that keeps your progress on
your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud, so it can follow
you to a new iPhone.

## What DuDu collects

**Nothing.** DuDu has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads and no
analytics. No data about you or your use of the app is ever sent to us or to
anyone else. The only place DuDu sends anything is your own iCloud, as described
under [iCloud sync](#icloud-sync) below.

## What DuDu stores, and where

Everything DuDu remembers is written to a private storage area inside the app's
own sandbox on your device, readable only by DuDu. That includes the name you
give yourself, your weekly goal and session lengths, the warm-ups and stretches
you finish (when, how long, which moves and how you felt), the routines you
build, your capsules, stickers and stardust, and your language, appearance,
voice, sound and haptics choices.

That copy is removed when you delete the app, or when you choose
Profile → Reset progress. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, it may be included
in your device backup; that backup is governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not ours.

## iCloud sync

From version 1.1.0, DuDu also keeps your progress in your iCloud account, so
every iPhone or iPad signed in to it has the same progress, and a new device
picks up where the old one left off.

- **What is synced:** your sessions (the warm-ups and stretches you finish),
  your custom routines, your settings (including your name, weekly goal and
  language), and your rewards (capsules, stickers and
  stardust).
- **Where it goes:** Apple's iCloud, into the *private* database of your own
  iCloud account, in a container only DuDu uses
  (`iCloud.com.minhthang.dudu`). We, the developer, cannot see or read it. The
  contents are stored in encrypted fields, protected with keys from your iCloud
  Keychain (end-to-end encryption). Storage counts toward your iCloud storage
  and is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **No one else:** no analytics, no ads and no third parties are involved. The
  data goes from your device to your iCloud and back to your other devices, and
  nowhere else.
- **Push notifications:** to hear that another of your devices changed your
  progress, iCloud sends DuDu silent push notifications through Apple's push
  service. They carry none of your data. The push token is managed by iOS and
  iCloud; DuDu never reads it or sends it anywhere.
- **Your choice:** sync is on when you are signed in to iCloud. Turn it off in
  DuDu under Profile → Settings → iCloud sync, or for all of iCloud under
  Settings → your name → iCloud. To delete DuDu's copy in iCloud, first choose
  Profile → Reset progress in DuDu, then use Settings → your name → iCloud →
  Manage Storage → DuDu Badminton → Delete Data. Resetting without turning sync
  off removes the data from your other devices too, as the reset syncs like any
  other change.

## Voice coach

The voice that reads out each move uses Apple's built-in speech synthesis,
which runs on your device. Nothing you do in DuDu is sent to Apple, to us or to
anyone else to produce it.

## Device language

DuDu reads your device's language setting so it can start in English or
Vietnamese without asking. That value is read locally and never leaves the
device. If you pick a language inside DuDu, that choice is stored on the device
too, and synced with your other settings if iCloud sync is on.

## Links to this website

Profile → Settings in the app has links to this privacy policy and to the
support page. Tapping one opens Safari; DuDu itself sends nothing for it. This website
is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the information any website
receives when you visit it, such as your IP address, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Tracking

DuDu does not track you. It contains no advertising identifiers, no third-party
SDKs that collect data and nothing that links your activity to other apps or websites.

## Children

DuDu collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Anything
synced stays in the private iCloud of the account the device is signed in to.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page

## Tóm tắt bằng tiếng Việt

DuDu Badminton **không thu thập bất kỳ dữ liệu nào**. Ứng dụng không có máy
chủ riêng, không có tài khoản, không quảng cáo và không phân tích. Mọi thứ (tên,
mục tiêu, các buổi tập, bài tập tự tạo, hộp quà, nhãn dán và cài đặt) được lưu
trong bộ nhớ riêng của ứng dụng trên thiết bị của bạn và bị xóa khi bạn xóa ứng
dụng hoặc chọn Hồ sơ → Xóa tiến trình.

Từ phiên bản 1.1.0, DuDu còn đồng bộ tiến trình (các buổi tập, bài tập tự tạo,
cài đặt, hộp quà, nhãn dán và bụi sao) vào **iCloud riêng tư của chính bạn** để
các thiết bị cùng đăng nhập iCloud có cùng dữ liệu. Dữ liệu được mã hóa đầu cuối
và nhà phát triển không thể xem. Không có bên thứ ba nào tham gia. Bạn có thể tắt
đồng bộ trong Hồ sơ → Cài đặt → Đồng bộ iCloud. Để xóa dữ liệu, chọn Hồ sơ → Xóa
tiến trình trong ứng dụng, rồi xóa bản sao trong Cài đặt iPhone → tên của bạn →
iCloud → Quản lý dung lượng. Giọng hướng dẫn dùng bộ đọc giọng nói có sẵn của
Apple, chạy ngay trên máy. DuDu không theo dõi bạn.
