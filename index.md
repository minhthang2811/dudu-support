# DuDu Badminton

Warm up before you play. Stretch after. DuDu the duck makes both feel like a
game.

DuDu Badminton is an iPhone and iPad coach for the two habits that keep
badminton players on court. Guided warm-ups take about four minutes and fit in
one step of space beside a crowded court; cool-downs come with a breathing
guide for every hold. A countdown ring, a voice coach, sounds and haptics lead
every move, so you never have to look at the screen.

Every warm-up and stretch earns a DuDu capsule with one of 12 outfits inside,
from a nón lá to an áo dài. Nothing is for sale: you earn everything by looking
after your body. A forgiving weekly streak, 18 badges and 39 moves that level
up from New to Gold keep you coming back.

Your progress stays on your device and in your own private iCloud, never on our
servers. In English and Tiếng Việt.
