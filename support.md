## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only. DuDu makes no network requests and has no account system.
See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

**How long does a warm-up take?**
Pick Quick, Standard or Full on Today. Quick takes about four minutes, and every
move fits in one step of space beside the court. You can also change the break
between moves on any routine, from No break to a minute.

**How do I earn capsules?**
Finish a warm-up (+1), finish a stretch (+1), do both on the same day (+1) and
reach each new best streak week (+1). Open them in Collection. Petting DuDu once
a day on Today earns stardust, which you can use to craft a sticker you are
missing. Nothing in DuDu is for sale.

**What are the odds in a capsule?**
They are shown in the app: Collection → the % button next to Open a capsule.
An Epic or better sticker is guaranteed within 10 capsules, and a Legendary one
within 40.

**How does the streak work?**
Set a weekly goal on Profile, from 1 to 7 days. Meet it and your streak grows by
a week. Shields protect a missed week, so one busy week does not cost you
everything.

**Can I make my own routine?**
Yes. Today → My routines → New routine. Pick any moves, drag them into your own
order, and set a timer for each. Customize on any routine starts from a copy.

**The voice coach is silent.**
Check Profile → Settings → Voice coach and Sounds. During a guided session DuDu
plays through the silent switch so you can hear the coach; elsewhere, sounds
follow the switch.

**How do I change the language?**
Profile → Settings → Language, or the EN / VI button on Today.

**Will my progress survive deleting the app?**
No. Deleting DuDu removes its storage with it.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. DuDu is a warm-up and stretching guide. Stop if anything hurts, and talk to
a professional about pain or injuries.
